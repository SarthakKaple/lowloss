# 📈 LowLoss TradeSim

**Let Profits Run. Manage the Risk.**

## 📌 About the Project

**LowLoss TradeSim** is a Python-based virtual stock trading and risk-management simulator focused on helping users understand and experiment with intelligent exit strategies.

Unlike a basic paper-trading simulator, LowLoss TradeSim explores dynamic profit targets and trailing stop-loss mechanisms that adapt to favourable price movements.

The simulator aims to allow users to configure initial profit targets and stop-loss levels, extend targets as prices rise, and track the highest price reached to dynamically adjust trailing exit thresholds.

It also aims to support two operating modes:

- **Alert Mode:** Notifies users when predefined trading conditions are met, allowing them to make the final decision.
- **Automatic Mode:** Executes virtual buy/sell actions when configured conditions are triggered.

The primary objective is to provide a risk-free environment to experiment with trading strategies, understand downside risk, and explore ways of protecting accumulated gains.

## 🎯 Core Objectives

1. Explore dynamic profit-target extensions instead of relying exclusively on fixed targets.
2. Implement trailing stop-loss mechanisms that adjust according to favourable price movements.
3. Help users understand how predefined trading rules can manage downside risk and protect accumulated gains.
4. Provide alert-based and automatic virtual trading modes.
5. Allow users to experiment with different target increments and trailing distances.
6. Analyse how different exit strategies affect simulated trading performance.

## 🤖 Long-Term AI Vision

One of the long-term goals of LowLoss TradeSim is to develop an AI-assisted trading system capable of executing buying and selling operations according to the user's instructions, selected strategies, and predefined risk limits.

The intended system would:

- Process the trading information and instructions provided by the user.
- Follow user-defined target and trailing stop-loss conditions.
- Automate simulated buying and selling decisions.
- Provide greater transparency about why a particular action was triggered.
- Keep the user in control of the strategy and its risk parameters.

The aim is to build a system that assists users in managing trading risks rather than blindly making financial decisions on their behalf.

## ❤️ My Inspiration

The inspiration behind LowLoss TradeSim comes from a personal experience: my mother's trading account, which experienced a loss of approximately **₹95,000 on an investment of ₹2,00,000**.

Seeing this made me think about how important risk management, trading discipline, and predefined exit strategies are.

I started this project with the hope of exploring ways to help people better understand and manage the risks involved in stock trading.

I know that losses cannot be completely eliminated, but I want to explore how technology can help people avoid unnecessary risks and make more informed decisions.

## ⚠️ Disclaimer

LowLoss TradeSim is an educational project currently under development. Its initial version uses virtual money and simulated transactions.

It does not currently connect to real stock markets, execute actual trades, or provide financial advice.

Any future AI-assisted or automated trading functionality will require extensive testing, appropriate safeguards, and careful consideration of financial risks. No system can guarantee profits or eliminate market losses.
